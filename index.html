To My Beautiful Girl,

Happy 20th Birthday.

It's crazy to think that we've been together for over a year now. Sometimes it feels like time has flown by, and other times it feels like I've known you forever.

This past year hasn't always been easy. We've had our ups and downs, our disagreements, our moments where things felt difficult, and moments where life seemed to be pulling us in different directions. But what means the most to me is that no matter what happened, we always chose each other. We always found our way back. Through every challenge, every conversation, every tear, every laugh, and every memory, we've continued to stand by one another.

You have become such a huge part of my life. You're the person I want to tell everything to first. You're the person who makes the good days better and somehow makes the hard days easier. You've seen me at my best and at my worst, and you've loved me through both.

I don't think you realize how much you've changed my life. The way you care, the way you love, the way you make me smile without even trying, those are things I'll never take for granted. Being with you has taught me what it means to truly love someone, not just when everything is perfect, but even when things are messy and difficult.

As you turn 20, I want you to know how proud I am of the person you've become. You are strong, kind, beautiful, caring, and capable of so much more than you give yourself credit for. I know life hasn't always been easy on you, but you've continued to push forward with a strength that inspires me every day.

Thank you for every late-night conversation, every hug when I needed one, every laugh, every adventure, every memory, and every moment you've shared with me. Thank you for loving me even when I wasn't easy to love. Thank you for being patient with me, believing in me, and choosing me.

I don't know exactly what the future holds for us, but I do know this: no matter what we've gone through, we've always found our way back to each other. And that's something I'll always cherish.

Today is all about you. I hope you feel loved, celebrated, appreciated, and reminded of just how special you are not only to me, but to everyone lucky enough to know you.

Happy 20th Birthday, my love.

Thank you for the most beautiful year of my life so far. I can't wait to make more memories with you, laugh with you, grow with you, and continue writing our story together.

I love you. Always.
